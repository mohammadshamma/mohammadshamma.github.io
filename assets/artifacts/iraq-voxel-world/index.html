    Navigating the 3D Voxel Atlas of Iraq

    This dual-scale voxel diorama projects Iraq’s geographic borders (29.1°N–37.4°N, 38.8°E–48.5°E), the Tigris, Euphrates (Hindiyah & Hillah branches), Shatt al-Arab, eight major lakes and reservoirs, the UNESCO Al-Ahwar Marshes, and 16 hand-crafted 0.25-scale micro-voxel heritage sites.

    Camera & Keyboard Controls

    • Orbit / Tilt: Left-drag on canvas or Q / E keys
    • Pan: Right-drag or WASD keys
    • Zoom: Scroll wheel or + / -
    • Tour Stops: [ and ] cycle through all 16 landmarks; R resets to Overview; Esc closes cards
