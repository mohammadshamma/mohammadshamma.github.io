    Palestine 3D Voxel Atlas

    This dual-scale 3D voxel diorama reconstructs historic Palestine (29.45°N–33.35°N) from Ras al-Naqura and the intact Buhayrat al-Hula papyrus marshes (+70 m) down the sub-sea-level Al-Ghawr rift (Buhayrat Tabariyya at −210 m and unified Al-Bahr al-Mayyit with Al-Lisan at −400 m) to Al-Naqab and Umm al-Rashrash on Khalij al-Aqaba.

    Five cartographic lenses highlight natural Palestinian landscapes (sanasel olive terraces, Marj Ibn Amer wheat plains, and coastal citrus bayyarat), perennial rivers, hypsometric elevation, all 16 Aqdiya across 6 Alwiya, and architectural eras across 19 hand-sculpted 0.25-scale micro-voxel landmarks.
